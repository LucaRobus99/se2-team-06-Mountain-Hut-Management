# 3. GENERAL ASSESSMENT

## 3.1 What caused your errors in estimation (if any)?

Our estimation accuracy was generally good, with only a small difference between estimated and actual working hours. However, some tasks took longer than expected, particularly testing, code reviews and integration. One of the main issues was giving too little weight to unit testing and too much emphasis to E2E testing. Additionally, some requirements and clarifications were still open after planning had been completed, while the Definition of Done was still evolving. This led to additional details and activities that had not been fully considered during the initial estimation.

## 3.2 What lessons did you learn (both positive and negative) in this sprint?

We learned that strong collaboration, communication and flexibility are essential to successfully completing a sprint, even when facing personal difficulties, limited availability or unexpected challenges. Despite these obstacles, we managed to remain cohesive as a team and deliver all committed work. We also learned not to get discouraged by problems that initially seem unsolvable and to adapt when necessary.

On the negative side, we realized that excessive stress can affect individual performance and that too much time was spent on planning and organizational activities. Furthermore, we understood the importance of regularly verifying and closing issues, since some progress charts only reflect completed issues rather than logged working hours.

## 3.3 Which improvement goals set in the previous retrospective were you able to achieve?

We made progress in several areas, particularly time tracking, task estimation, organization and coordination. We recorded working hours more consistently in YouTrack and improved our estimations by taking organizational and coordination activities into account. We also managed to speed up work on tasks that had already been addressed in the previous sprint.

Overall, we believe we achieved most, if not all, of our previous improvement goals, resulting in more accurate planning and better teamwork.

## 3.4 Which ones were you not able to achieve? Why?

Although we made progress towards our improvement goals, some aspects still need further attention. In particular, maintaining a consistent working pace on new tasks, especially testing-related ones, remains challenging. Our planning and coordination activities can also be further optimized to reduce the time spent on organizational matters.

Therefore, while the previous goals were largely achieved, we recognize that these improvements require continuous effort.

## 3.5 Improvement goals for the next sprint and how to achieve them (technical tasks, team coordination, etc.)

Our main goals for the next sprint are to improve planning efficiency, maintain a more consistent development pace and make progress more visible throughout the sprint.

To achieve this, we will try to send and resolve clarifications as early as possible, ideally before Sprint Planning begins. We will also allocate more time to development and testing by improving testing estimations and including a small buffer based on our previous experience.

Finally, we aim to follow the ideal Burndown more closely, particularly in the Issue Count and Estimation charts, by completing verification earlier, avoiding leaving issues in the Verify state for too long and closing them as soon as they meet the Definition of Done.

## 3.6 One thing you are proud of as a Team!

We are proud of our ability to work together and successfully complete all the stories we committed to, while staying within our estimated working hours. Despite personal difficulties, limited availability and occasional communication and organizational challenges, we managed to maintain a collaborative and supportive environment.

We also demonstrated flexibility, good organization and the ability to manage stress constructively, turning potential difficulties into opportunities to improve our teamwork and achieve our common goals.
